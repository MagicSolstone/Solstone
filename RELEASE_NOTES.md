# Solstone 1.4.0

## One site
- **The website is always one click away.** Inside the app, a bar along the top has Home, News, Guides, FAQ and Live status.
- **Signing out takes you to the home page.**
- **Signed in, the home page knows you.** It shows a **Servers** link and your name instead of *Sign in*, both going straight to the servers.

## Music is gone
- The music player, library, playlists, radio, its settings and the bar along the bottom have been removed. It was a lot to look after, it used your home internet, and sharing music has copyright problems. Your music files aren't touched. Solstone's own copy of uploads in `/data/music` can be deleted.

## Fireworks
- **A small fireworks control in the corner** replaces the bottom bar. It has four buttons:
  - **Launch** opens a list: *Surprise me*, every kind of firework with a picture, and the fireworks players made for the show. Tap as many as you like.
  - **Show** turns on fireworks by themselves.
  - **🔊** turns firework sounds on and off.
  - **👁** hides the page so only the sky is left.
- **The home page has fireworks too.** They're off until a visitor turns the show on, and each browser remembers its own choice.
- **The firework editor is simpler.** Saved fireworks open on **Mine**, and *Everyone's* is grouped by who made them. Collections are gone: no field, no folders, no menus on each firework.

## Your logo
- **Website → Site details → Logo**: upload a picture (PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, under 1 MB). It replaces Solstone's logo beside the name and in the browser tab, everywhere. *Use Solstone's* puts the old one back.

## Easier to read
- The pixel font is used only for big titles. Tabs, server descriptions, player names, small headings and banners use the clear body font.
- The pixel font's broken "fi" ligature is off, so "firework" no longer shows as "Arework".

## Smaller fixes
- **Website → Posts**: the explanation of the formatting sits above the text box instead of pushing it off to the side.
- **Status page**: *Lately* lines up with the first card instead of the tabs.
- **Server tab**: it opens on Performance, then Mods, then Chat.
- **Mods**: Floodgate, Geyser, Fabric API, Lithium and other well-known mods get a description when their files don't have one.
